Files in this folder are **not part of the plugin ZIP** (excluded in `.distignore`). After the plugin is approved they go into the SVN `assets/` folder, not `trunk/`.

Guidance for the icon and banner: original artwork only; no other plugin's logo, colours copied from a competitor, or trademarked symbols; legible at 128 px; works on light and dark backgrounds.
